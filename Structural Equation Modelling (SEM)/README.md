###### Introduction ######

Finance plays a vital role in the social-economy sector, especially in financial well-being. Financial behaviour of individuals contributes to the financial health of local communities and a whole country. In 2016, the US government collaborated with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) department to implement the financial well-being survey [14] to understand the financial management of households.
 
This project uses the data from the survey and applies the theory of structural equation model (SEM). The project starts from section 1 with the introduction of the research question as why the financial well-being is essential part for household and a whole country, and why SEM is an appropriate application to investigate this empirical case. The brief of data set, and measuring of latent variables will be presented in section 2. The methodology and hypotheses applying in the project are introduced in section 3. In section 4, we conduct the specific approach of SEM in terms of confirmation factor analysis (CFA), measurement invariance, multiple indicators and multiple causes (MIMIC), and SEM in multi-group analysis. The project is finalized with a conclusion and a suggestion for further research in section 5 and 6, respectively.

The goal of this rearch is to apply the method of corpus linguistics to the Coronavirus corpus. We will address the research question: how did people change their views about the Corona pandemic from before to after the lockdown. At different time points, our views about a certain event might be different. It is in the best interest of corpus linguistics to explore the impacts of a particular event on society, economy, and culture. The major statistical method conducted in the paper is correspondence analysis (CA). CA is used to analyze the dependence between two (or more) categorical variables. The categorical variables in corpus linguistics are words, text, phrases, etc. CA then will visualize categories of the variables as points in a low-dimension space to interpret the relationship between/among these variables. We, therefore, aim to see if there are existing varieties in the texts and words used before and after the lockdown.

@ Programming: R, Rmardown, and Quarto. 

@ Methods: Corresponding Analysis, Exploratory Data Analysis, Multi-variable Analysis.
